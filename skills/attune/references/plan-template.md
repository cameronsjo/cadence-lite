# Plan template

Use the repository's existing format where one exists. Otherwise retain these
fields so intro and outro can reconstruct the state without rereading a session.
Fill them with actual decisions; omit sections that do not help the task.

    # Plan title
    Status: proposed | approved | in progress | blocked | completed
    Next action: one concrete action, or none when completed

    ## Goal and completion criteria
    Observable outcome and scope, including relevant exclusions.

    ## Approach and alternatives
    Chosen approach, consequential decisions, and real alternatives declined.

    ## Work
    - [ ] Bounded step with its dependency and verification where needed.

    ## Review
    Roles used, examined revision, findings and dispositions, unavailable reviews.

    ## Completion evidence
    Commands run, results, what was exercised, and remaining unverified boundaries.

Update status and next action with the work. Checklist progress alone does not
prove completion; reconcile it with the actual change and verification.
